Hoofa Job Alerts Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Who we are

Hoofa Job Alerts is operated by Adam Pratt, an individual. Contact: hoofa@mail.grokbot.com.

Information we collect

Your mobile phone number, your opt-in and opt-out records (keyword, date and time), the content of messages you send us, and job-search preferences you provide (such as desired roles, locations, and pay).

How we use it

Only to deliver the Hoofa Job Alerts service: to send you matching job openings, answer your messages, honor HELP and STOP requests, and keep records of your consent. Information is processed only as needed to deliver the service. We do not sell your information.

SMS privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Retention and deletion

Reply STOP to stop messages. Text DELETE or email us to have your data deleted.
