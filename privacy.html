Hoofa job alerts – Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026. Hoofa job alerts is a personal SMS job-search assistant operated by Adam Pratt (sole proprietor).

Information we collect

Your mobile phone number, the text messages you send to and receive from the program, and job preferences you share (for example location, salary range, interests, and companies to exclude).

How we use it

Only to send you the job digest you signed up for, reply to your messages, and tailor job recommendations to your preferences. Message content is processed by our service providers (an SMS provider and an AI provider) solely to deliver the program.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We do not sell your information.

Retention and deletion

We keep your messages and preferences only while you are subscribed. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. To have your data deleted, text DELETE to the program number or contact the operator, and we will remove it within 30 days.

Contact

Reply HELP to the program number for help. See also our Terms and Conditions.
