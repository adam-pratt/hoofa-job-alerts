Hoofa job alerts – SMS Terms and Conditions

Effective October 11, 2026. Operated by Adam Pratt (sole proprietor).

Program description

Hoofa job alerts is a personal job-search assistant. Subscribers receive one daily text digest with up to three job openings matching their career preferences, plus replies to questions they text to the program.

How to opt in

Text START to (914) 860-3593. You will receive a confirmation message. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

About 1 message per day, plus replies to your messages.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply.

Help and opt-out

Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to cancel at any time; you will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe.
